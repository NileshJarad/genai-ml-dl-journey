# Deep Learning Interview Questions

## Technical Questions

1. **Backpropagation**: Walk me through backpropagation for a simple neural network. Show the forward pass and backward pass step by step.

2. **Activations**: Compare ReLU, tanh, and softmax. Which ones have gradients that vanish at initialization? Why?

3. **Batch Size Effects**: How does batch size affect gradient descent optimization? What are the optimal ranges and why?

4. **Dropout Mechanism**: Explain dropout as a regularization technique. How does it work during training vs. inference?

5. **CNN Architecture**: What makes a good CNN architecture? Compare Conv layers, pooling layers, and fully connected layers.

6. **Optimizers**: Compare SGD, Adam, and AdamW. What are the pros and cons of each optimizer?

7. **Weight Initialization**: How does weight initialization affect network training? Explain Xavier/Glorot and He initialization.

8. **Loss Functions**: Explain cross-entropy loss. How is it implemented for both classification and regression tasks?

9. **Data Pipeline**: What components make up a good neural network data pipeline? How do you handle data parallelism?

10. **Model Evaluation**: How would you evaluate a deep learning model for image classification? Which metrics are most appropriate?

11. **Pretraining**: Why is pretraining important? Describe pretraining objectives like contrastive learning and masked language modeling.

12. **Parallel Training**: How do data, model, and pipeline parallelism differ? When would you use each?

13. **Gradient Clipping**: When and why is gradient clipping necessary? What about gradient scaling?

14. **Embedding Layers**: Explain how word embeddings work. How do they improve NLP tasks?

15. **Hyperparameters**: What are the key hyperparameters in deep learning? How do you systematically tune them?

## Algorithm Questions

16. **Autoencoders**: What is an autoencoder? How do autoencoders differ from PCA? What's their advantage for dimensionality reduction?

17. **RNN vs LSTM**: What makes LSTM different from vanilla RNN? How do gated cells work for sequence modeling?

18. **GANs**: What are Generative Adversarial Networks? Explain the generator-discriminator relationship.

19. **AlphaFold**: Describe AlphaFold's approach to protein structure prediction. Why is it significant in bioinformatics?

20. **Generative AI**: How do diffusion models work for image generation? Describe the noise-to-data process.

## Domain Knowledge

21. What are transformer architectures and why are they dominant in modern AI?

22. Explain the concept of fine-tuning language models. How does LoRA work?

23. What are retrieval-augmented generation systems? Why are they important?

24. What role does attention play in LLM architecture?

25. How do quantization and pruning benefit deep learning models?

26. What is the importance of Redundancy Analysis in neural networks?

27. How do reinforcement learning and supervised learning differ fundamentally?

28. What are functional programming paradigms in relation to ML frameworks?
